2. Information We May Collect

In general, we may collect personal information directly from:

· Users of the connected vehicle services.

· Your vehicle.

Information Collected from Users of the Vehicle Service 

We may collect personal Information directly from you, such as when you activate the connected vehicle services or communicate with us related to the connected vehicle services.

·  Activation: When activating the connected vehicle services, we may collect information that you provide us such as your email address, region, and other contact information.

· Customer Service and Support Data: When you request for information, calls, emails, or communications, we will maintain records about our interactions and communications with you in the form of telephone voice, online interactive text, or other forms, including the nature of the request, your name and contact information, and information related to our resolution of your request.

Information Collected from Your Connected Vehicle 

Your vehicle is equipped with various software, sensors, features, and other technologies that may generate, store, transmit or permit the retrieval of information from a vehicle, which includes information related to the performance, location, operation and use of your vehicle and the connected vehicle services. When the connected vehicle services are available and enabled, your vehicle or we will collect your information:

· Vehicle Identifiable Data: Each vehicle has a unique vehicle identification number (VIN). There are other similar vehicle identifiable number, such as vehicle host device ID, vehicle integrate circuit card identity (ICCID) and vehicle international mobile equipment identity (IMEI). Generally, these vehicle identifiable data are not transmitted to the service providers, for example,  voice assistant service provider Cerence B. V. , as listed below. These data are only collected if the related services in your vehicle have been activated.

· Vehicle Usage and Performance Data: Information related to the use of your vehicle and the connected vehicle services, as well as vehicle performance, usage, operation, health, diagnostic logs, charging information and service-related data. This includes information such as odometer, mileage, and emissions data; tire pressure data; trouble or error codes, and other diagnostic data; brake status; engine performance;  fuel levels and refueling activity; battery levels and status; vehicle settings, commands and presets, points of interest, and other information about your use of certain features; Wi-Fi password, Wi-Fi security mode, Wi-Fi SSID, Bluetooth name and other performance data; as well as associated date and time stamps for such information. These data can be stored locally in your vehicle and remain inaccessible by us unless a specific event is triggered by your request service or repairs. For example, when you use our car control and monitoring services through BYD APP, we collect your personal information, including system version, platform version, vehicle door and vehicle door lock status, power gear, status of vehicle windows and sunroofs, tire pressure status, engine coolant temperature, total mileage, vehicle system warning status, oil pressure indicator light  status, 50km fuel consumption, 200m average instantaneous power consumption, meter fault indicator status, charging plan,charging status, air conditioner type, remaining charging time, whether the air conditioner support temperature regulation, time for remote control of air conditioner, control results of  air conditioner, air conditioner comfort color, PM2.5 concentrations inside and outside the vehicle, driver's seat ventilation and heating status, front passenger's seat  ventilation and heating status, rear seats ventilation and heating status, fuel consumption, cumulative average fuel consumption, charging status, SOC, vehicle light status, in-car temperature, vital signs detection warning, vehicle type, and steering wheel heating status, battery preheating status, battery preheating time, battery temperature, charging voltage, scheduled charging plan, local time on the vehicle screen, scheduled driving plan.

· Vehicle Driving Data: We collect driving data about your vehicle such as speed, acceleration, and braking data; direction of travel; trip data (mileage, date, length, conditions); ignition events; steering events; cruise control data; seatbelt status; information about vehicle incidents or events; other information about how you drive a vehicle; as well as associated date and time stamps for such information. These data can be stored locally in your vehicle and remain inaccessible by us unless a specific event is triggered by your request service or repairs. For example, when you use our car control and monitoring services through BYD APP, we will collect your personal information, including 50km fuel consumption, total mileage, 200m average instantaneous power consumption, and speed.

· Vehicle Geolocation Data: In providing the connected vehicle services, we may collect geolocation information, including your vehicle’s GPS location. When you engage remote services using your mobile device, we also collect your mobile device location information. Geolocation information may be associated with the other information, such as photo image, which are stored locally in your vehicle. To protect your privacy, geolocation information is either processed directly without leaving your vehicle or transmitted directly to the service provider in a form that does not directly identify you.

· Diagnostic data: We collect your vehicle operation diagnostic logs for identifying and troubleshooting unexpected software or connectivity issues, and other debugging log reports. Diagnostics may include details about vehicle configuration, firmware, energy use, electronic systems state, and other system-to-system data used to identify bugs and conduct technical evaluations. The readout is generally carried out via the on-board diagnostics (OBD) or the statutorily prescribed connection. These data are stored locally in your vehicle and remain inaccessible by us unless a specific event is triggered by your request service or repairs.

· Infotainment system data: Your vehicle may collect your vehicle infotainment system analytics data regarding infotainment usage such as function successfully or unsuccessfully loaded, aggregated counts of the features or apps used, playback and browsing metadata. These infotainment features can be stored locally, or they can be located on a device that you have linked with the vehicle (e.g., smartphone, USB stick or SD card). These data are only transmitted out of the vehicle  at your request, particularly as part of the use of services in line with the settings selected by you.

· Voice commands: Your vehicle is equipped with the ability for you to use your voice to speak commands to the vehicle. We don’t capture your voice record.

· Digital Key service: We collect your vehicle identification number (VIN) to provide Digital Key service.

· Advanced driver assistance system data: Your vehicle may have been equipped with a camera suite, providing advanced features such as driver monitor system(DMS)，driving assist and advanced driver assistance system to recognize things like lane lines and street signs. Advanced driver assistance system data from the camera suite is processed directly in your vehicle.

· Child Presence Detection(CPD):  In order to detect the presence of a child in the vehicle, we may detect information about the breathing,body movements, or any other sign of life in the vehicle; please note that we do not store this information.

· Intrusive detection services data: Your vehicle may be installed with intrusion detection and prevention system(IDPS) to provide continuous protection for vehicle from cyber attacks by detecting in-vehicle attacks, monitoring fleet-wide security events, and uploading to the cloud. In order to implement such function, VIN, vehicle intranet IP address and components serial number will be collected. Please note that the IDPS is based on legal obligation.

· Cellular Data Usage. The in-vehicle application platform needs to consume cellular data when connecting to the network. In order to facilitate the settlement of data usage fees, we need to identify your VIN, data package, and remaining data usage. In order to provide troubleshooting and analysis services, we need to record data usage (including data usage by each application).

The type of other data processing is determined by the provider of the app used. Whether and which settings you can adjust depends on the relevant app and your smartphone’s operating system.

We may also use and disclose information about you if we believe in good faith that that the law requires it for purposes of security, fulfilling our legal obligations, law enforcement, or other issues of public importance, disclosure is necessary or appropriate. We may also share information about you, where there are legal grounds to do so, if we determine that disclosure is reasonably necessary to enforce our other terms or agreements between you and us or the service providers  or protect our operations or customers. This could include providing information to public or governmental authorities. Additionally, in the event of a reorganization, merger, change of control, or sale we may transfer any personal information we collect to the relevant service provider.

Except as described here, we may also collect, use, and share information that does not, on its own, personally identify you. Such information may be used for any purpose, including for example, for operational or research purposes, for industry analysis, to improve or modify our products and services, to better tailor our products and services to your needs, and where legally required.
