1. What data do we collect?

To provide our services, we have a need to process personal data. We typically process the following personal data when you use our products and services:

A. From your BYD Vehicle:

· Vehicle Operation and After-sales Service: In order to fulfill our legal obligations, ensure the continuous operation of the vehicle, conduct safety analysis, identify and solve potential problems, and investigate accidents, we need to collect and process vehicle data related to the use, operation and condition of the vehicle through the on-board sensor equipment supporting the vehicle products. The vehicle data contains vehicle VIN code, vehicle driving status data (such as gears, motor speed, battery life, etc.), car body basic data (such as electronic stability system working status, etc.), electrical equipment working status data (such as A/C working status, car doors and windows unlocking status, seat adjustment switch working status, etc.), auxiliary driving working status, entertainment system working status, etc. We may collect such information via in-person during a service appointment or remote access.

· Auxiliary Driving: In order to implement the auxiliary function such as auxiliary driving and auxiliary reversing, the car side will use the vehicle's front, flanks, and rear cameras to collect image data on the road during driving or after parking, image data surrounding the vehicle (e.g., street, lane lines, traffic signs, parking signs, etc.) to complete vehicle auxiliary driving function.

· Intrusion Detection and Prevention Services:  Your vehicle may be installed with intrusion detection and prevention system (IDPS) to provide continuous protection for vehicle from cyberattacks by detecting in-vehicle attacks, monitoring fleet-wide security events, and uploading to the cloud. In order to implement such function, we will collect VIN, Vehicle Intranet IP Address and components serial number. Please note that we will not store these data.

· Camera Detection: Your vehicle may be equipped with camera suites, providing advanced features such as Driver Monitor System (DMS) which may analyse your behaviours, including your eyebrow and eyelids movement and facial characteristics to determine if you are dizzy while driving and push warning notification.  Please note that we will not store these data.

· Child Presence Detection(CPD):  In order to detect the presence of a child in the vehicle, we may detect information about the breathing, body movements, or any other sign of life in the vehicle; please note that we do not store this information.

· Software Update: In order to optimize the vehicle status and provide secure software upgrade services, we need to identify your vehicle's VIN code, system version, upgrade history, vehicle status, and other data.

· Third-party service: In order to better provide service of telematics, when you access the third-party services, the third party will comply with its privacy statement and be responsible for the personal data collected and processed by them. Please refer to the user agreement and privacy statement provided on the software. We will also conduct due diligence to review the personal data protection capabilities of third parties to ensure that they provide services in compliance with legal and regulatory requirements. At the same time, in order to protect your legitimate rights and interests, we suggest that you immediately terminate relevant operations and contact us in a timely manner when you discover any risks arising from third-party services.

· Bluetooth and Wi-Fi: In order to implement the functions of Bluetooth and Wi-Fi, when you connect the car with other devices via Bluetooth or Wi-Fi, we will record the device information (such as Mac address、Wi-Fi password, Wi-Fi security mode, Wi-Fi SSID, Bluetooth name) linked by Bluetooth or Wi-Fi.

· Cellular Data Usage: The in-vehicle application platform needs to consume cellular data when connecting to the network. In order to facilitate the settlement of data usage fees, we need to identify your VIN, data package, and remaining data usage. In order to provide troubleshooting and analysis services, we need to record data usage (including data usage by each application).

· Voice commands: Your vehicle is equipped with the ability for you to use your voice to speak commands to the vehicle. We don’t capture your voice record.

· Digital Key service: We collect your vehicle identification number (VIN) to provide Digital Key service.

· E-call Service: In the event of a traffic accident and roadside assistance, the E-call system on the in-vehicle terminal will provide your vehicle location, accident information and contact information to the agency. The collection and sharing of these data comply with the requirements of laws and regulations. We only send Minimal Set of Data (MSD) to the related agency when there is an alarm.

· Service history data: In order to facilitate the service of your car, we may collect and process data about the service history of each BYD vehicle, including customer’s name, phone number, email address, vehicle identification number (VIN), purchase history and any other information related to its service history.

· Telematics log data: To improve our vehicles and services for you, we may collect certain telematics data regarding the performance, usage, operation, and condition of your BYD vehicle, including:

o Vehicle Identifiable Information, including vehicle identification number (VIN), vehicle host device ID, vehicle integrate circuit card identity (ICCID) and vehicle international mobile equipment identity (IMEI);

o Vehicle status information, including speed, battery and charging, charging plan, charging status, odometer readings, conditions of car seats, doors, locks and windows, power gear, A/C system, tire pressure system, remaining fuel information, car lamps status, ECU(Electronic Control Unit) information.

o Cellular data usage, Mac address, Wi-Fi password, Wi-Fi security mode, Wi-Fi SSID, Bluetooth name;

o Other data to assist in identifying issues and analyzing the performance of the vehicle.

 

B. On site

· When you visit a BYD store/dealer store or BYD service facility, attend one of our events, sign up for a test drive, or sign a purchase contract with us, we or our dealers may collect information from or about you offline, including:

o Your basic information: your name, plate number.

o Contact information: your address, phone number, email address.

o Vehicle information: vehicle identification number (VIN), user ID, vehicle plate number, vehicle model, hardware ID, vehicle telematics log data, repair history, etc.

o Purchase information:  purchase date, delivery status.

6. What rights do you have in relation to your data?

Unless exceptions provided by applicable law and regulations, you generally have the right to ask us:

· for access to and a copy of your personal data that we hold

· that some of your personal data is provided to you or sent to another database manager in a commonly used, machine readable format.

· to update or correct your personal data in order to make it accurate.

· to delete your personal data from our records in certain circumstances.

· to restrict the processing of your personal data in certain circumstances.

And you may also have a right:

· to object to us processing your personal data in certain circumstances (e.g. in case we process your data for direct marketing purposes.

Disabling location-based services: If you no longer wish us to collect your location information, or your address information, please disable them in “Car Settings-Settings- App- Permissions- Location Information” in your vehicle screen. Please note that if you disable this function, we cannot provide with you map navigation service.

If the processing of personal data is based on the consent of the Data Subject, he or she may withdraw his or her consent at any time, but this does not affect the lawfulness of the previous processing.

These rights may be limited in some situations – for example, where we can demonstrate that we have a legal requirement to process your data or if making the information available to you would reveal personal data about another person or if we are legally prevented from disclosing such information. In some instances, this may mean that we are able to retain data even if you withdraw your consent.

We hope that we can satisfy any of your rights regarding your data. If you have any concerns about how we process your data, you can contact us as described below in the section “If you have any questions about your data”.

In the event you still have unresolved concerns, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority, in particular the data protection authority in the Member State of your habitual residence or place of work.

Finally, please note that where we require personal data to comply with legal or contractual obligations, then provision of such data is mandatory: if such data is not provided, then we will not be able to manage our contractual relationship, or to meet obligations placed on us. In all other cases, provision of requested personal data is optional.

7. How long do we retain your data?

For data about your purchasing our products or receiving our services, BYD will keep this data for legal and tax preservation obligations. Where you are a customer, BYD will keep your information for the duration of any contractual relationship you have with us, and, to the extent permitted, after the end of that relationship for as long as necessary to perform the purposes set out in this Statement.  We retain the information we collect from or about you for the period necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this Privacy Statement unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by law. When the information is no longer necessary for these purposes, we delete it or keep it in a form that does not identify you. When determining this retention period, we take into account various criteria, including the type of services requested by or provided to you, the nature of our relationship with you, the impact on the services we provide to you if we delete some information from or about you, and retention periods required by law.

8. How do we protect your data?

We strive to maintain the highest standards of security and BYD has put in place robust technical and organizational measures for the protection of your data in accordance with the current, general state of technology, especially to protect the data against loss, falsification or access by unauthorized third persons. However the transmission of information via the internet is not completely secure. So, whilst we will do our best to protect your personal data, we cannot guarantee the complete security of your data transmitted via BYD vehicle system. Any transmission is at your own risk. Once we have received your personal data we will use strict procedures and security features to prevent unauthorized access. In the unfortunate event that a personal data security incident occurs, we will report it promptly and take remedial measures in accordance with the requirements of the law and regulatory authorities.
