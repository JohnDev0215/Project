1. What data do we collect?

When we provide our services, we have a need to process personal data. We typically process the following personal data when you use our products and services:

(1) Vehicle Operation and After-sales Service: In order to fulfil our legal obligations, ensure the continuous operation of the vehicle, conduct safety analysis, identify and solve potential problems, and investigate accidents, we need to collect and process vehicle data related to the use, operation and condition of the vehicle through the on-board sensor equipment supporting the vehicle products. The vehicle data contains vehicle VIN code, vehicle driving status data (such as gears, motor speed, battery life, etc.), car body basic data (such as electronic stability system working status, etc.), electrical equipment working status data (such as A/C working status, car doors and windows unlocking status, seat adjustment switch working status, etc.), auxiliary driving working status, entertainment system working status, etc.

(2) Auxiliary Driving: In order to implement the auxiliary function such as auxiliary driving and auxiliary reversing, the car side will use the vehicle's front, flanks, and rear cameras to collect image data on the road during driving or after parking, image data surrounding the vehicle (e.g., street, lane lines, traffic signs, parking signs, etc.) and vehicle location data, to complete vehicle auxiliary driving function.

(3) Software Update: In order to optimize the vehicle status and provide secure software upgrade services, we need to identify your vehicle's VIN code, system version, upgrade history, vehicle status, and other data.

(4) Third-party service: In order to better provide service of telematics, when you access the third-party services such as music, maps, and voice assistant (subject to the services provided by the on-board intelligence systems), the third party will comply with its privacy statement and be responsible for the personal data collected and processed by them. Please refer to the user agreement and privacy statement provided on the software. We will also conduct due diligence to review the personal data protection capabilities of third parties to ensure that they provide services in compliance with legal and regulatory requirements. At the same time, in order to protect your legitimate rights and interests, we suggest that you immediately terminate relevant operations and contact us in a timely manner when you discover any risks arising from third-party services.

(5) Bluetooth and Wi-Fi: In order to implement the functions of Bluetooth and Wi-Fi, when you connect the car with other devices via Bluetooth or Wi-Fi, we will record the device information (such as Mac address、Wi-Fi password, Wi-Fi security mode, Wi-Fi SSID, Bluetooth name;) linked by Bluetooth or Wi-Fi.

(6) Cellular Data Usage: The in-vehicle application platform needs to consume cellular data when connecting to the network. In order to facilitate the settlement of data usage fees, we need to identify your VIN, data package, and remaining data usage. In order to provide troubleshooting and analysis services, we need to record data usage (including data usage by each application).

(7) eCall Service: (Please note that this service is now only available in our vehicles in the United Arab Emirates in Middle East & Africa countries) In the event when you are in a traffic accident or you press the SOS button for roadside assistance, the eCall system on the in-vehicle terminal will provide your vehicle location,contact information and additional required information to the PSAP (Public Safety Answering Point which is set up by government authority). The collection and sharing of these data comply with the requirements of laws and regulations, which only include Minimal Set of Data (MSD), namely MSD message identifier, types of triggers, vehicle VIN code, vehicle power type, timestamp and other required information.

(8) Voice commands: Your vehicle is equipped with the ability for you to use your voice to speak commands to the vehicle. We don’t capture your voice record.

(9) Digital Key service: We collect your vehicle identification number (VIN) to provide Digital Key service.

(10) Intrusion Detection and Prevention Services:  Your vehicle may be installed with intrusion detection and prevention system (IDPS) to provide continuous protection for vehicle from cyberattacks by detecting in-vehicle attacks, monitoring fleet-wide security events, and uploading to the cloud. In order to implement such function, we will collect VIN, Vehicle Intranet IP Address and components serial number. Please note that the detection system is based on legal requirement.

(11) Camera Detection: Your vehicle may be equipped with camera suites, providing advanced features such as Driver Monitor System (DMS) which may analyse your behaviours, including your eyebrow and eyelids movement and facial characteristics to determine if you are dizzy while driving to push warning notification. Please note that such function is based on legal requirement.

(12) Child Presence Detection(CPD): In order to detect the presence of a child in the vehicle, we may detect information about the breathing,body movements, or any other sign of life in the vehicle; please note that we do not store this information.

(13) Telematics log data: To improve our vehicles and services for you, we may collect certain telematics data regarding the performance, usage, operation, and condition of your BYD vehicle, including:

· Vehicle status information, including system version, platform version,

· vehicle door and vehicle door lock status, power gear, status of vehicle windows and sunroofs, tire pressure status, engine coolant temperature, total mileage, vehicle system warning status, oil pressure indicator light status, 50km fuel consumption, 200m average instantaneous power consumption, meter fault indicator status, charging plan, charging status, air conditioner type, remaining charging time, whether the air conditioner support temperature regulation, time for remote control of air conditioner, control results of air conditioner, air conditioner comfort color, PM2.5 concentrations inside and outside the vehicle, driver's seat ventilation and heating status, front passenger's seat ventilation and heating status, rear seats ventilation and heating status, fuel consumption, cumulative average fuel consumption, charging status, SOC, vehicle light status, in-car temperature, vital signs detection warning, vehicle type, and steering wheel heating status, battery preheating status, battery preheating time, battery temperature, charging voltage, scheduled charging plan, local time on the vehicle screen, scheduled driving plan. .

· User email address, phone number，Cellular data usage；

· VIN code , vehicle integrate circuit card identity (ICCID) and vehicle international mobile equipment identity (IMEI); ;

· and other data to assist in identifying issues and analyzing the performance of the vehicle.

6. What rights do you have in relation to your data?

Unless exceptions provided by applicable law and regulations, you generally have the right to ask us:

(1) for access to and a copy of your personal data that we hold.

(2) that some of your personal data is provided to you or sent to another database controller in a commonly used, machine-readable format.

(3) to update or correct your personal data in order to make it accurate.

(4) to delete your personal data from our records in certain circumstances.

(5) to restrict the processing of your personal data in certain circumstances.

(6) to object to us processing your personal data in certain circumstances (e.g. in case we process your data for direct marketing purposes).

We are happy to assist you to exercise your legitimate rights. You can get in touch with us at any time via the contact information provided in Section 9. Please note that when you exercise the rights above, we may ask you to provide us with additional information, such as to confirm your identity.

However, these rights may be limited in some situations according to applicable data protection laws – for example, where we can demonstrate that we have a legal requirement to process your data or if making the information available to you would reveal personal data about another person or if we are legally prevented from disclosing such information. In some instances, this may mean that we are able to retain data even if you withdraw your consent.

In the event you still have unresolved concerns, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

7. How long do we retain your data?

For data about receiving our services, we will keep this data for legal and tax preservation obligations. Where you are a customer, we will keep your data for the duration of any contractual relationship you have with us, and, to the extent permitted, after the end of that relationship for as long as necessary to perform the purposes set out in this Privacy Statement.

We retain the data we collect from or about you for the period necessary to fulfil the purposes outlined in this Privacy Statement unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by law. When the data is no longer necessary for these purposes, we delete it or keep it in a form that does not identify you. When determining this retention period, we take into account various criteria, including the type of services requested by or provided to you, the nature of our relationship with you, the impact on the services we provide to you if we delete some data from or about you, and retention periods required by law.

8. How do we protect your data?

We strive to maintain the appropriate standards of security and we has put in place robust technical and organizational measures for the protection of your data in accordance with the current, general state of technology, especially to protect the data against loss, falsification or access by unauthorized third persons. However, the transmission of information via the internet is not completely secure. So, whilst we will do our best to protect your personal data, we cannot guarantee the 100% security of your data transmitted with us.

Once we have received your personal data we will use strict procedures and security features to prevent unauthorized access. In the unfortunate event that a personal data incident occurs, we will report it promptly and take remedial measures in accordance with the requirements of the law and regulatory authorities.
