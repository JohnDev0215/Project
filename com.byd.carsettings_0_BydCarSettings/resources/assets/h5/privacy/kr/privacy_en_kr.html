1. What data we collect

Your vehicle is equipped with various software, sensors, features, and other technologies that may generate, store, transmit or permit the retrieval of information from a vehicle, which includes information related to the performance, operation and use of your vehicle and the connected vehicle services. When the connected vehicle services are available and enabled, your vehicle or we will collect your information. Please notice that the provision of the service is subject to the actual function and configuration of the vehicle.

· Vehicle Type Data: vehicle identification number (VIN), in-vehicle device ID and vehicle configuration..

· Vehicle Status Data. performance and status of the vehicle, such as door lock status, gear status, window and sunroof status,tire pressure status, mileage, speed, battery temperature, State of Charge, Additionally, associated timestamps for such information are also collected.

· Network Connectivity Data: To provide the in-vehicle SIM card networking service, we will collect your IP address,,vehicle integrated circuit card identity (ICCID), and vehicle international mobile equipment identity (IMEI)，and remaining data usage, including data usage by each application.

· Wireless Connectivity Data: When you connect the vehicle with other devices via Bluetooth or Wi-Fi, we will collect the device information, including Mac address, Wi-Fi password, Wi-Fi security mode, Wi-Fi SSID, and the Bluetooth name linked by Bluetooth or Wi-Fi.

 

Additionally, we also collect the following data when you use particular functions/services equipped with the vehicle:

· Device Data: We may collect your device information including digital key ID, digital key password,  and your mobile system version,

· Child Presence Detection (CPD)-related Data: We may collect the door open/close status, information about breathing, body movements and other signs of life in vehicle,to detect whether a living object is present in the vehicle. Please note that such information cannot be used to identify a specific individual and  we do not store such information.

· Driver Monitor System (DMS)-related Data: Your vehicle may be equipped with camera suites that provide advanced features of the Driver Monitor System (DMS), which may collect your eyebrow and eyelids movements and facial characteristics to determine if you are drowsy while driving and to push warning notifications. We will not store this data. Please note that such functionality is based on legal requirements or best practice standards.

· Intrusion Detection and Prevention Services (IDPS)-related Data: Your vehicle may be equipped with an Intrusion Detection and Prevention System (IDPS) to provide continuous protection against cyberattacks by detecting in-vehicle threats, monitoring fleet-wide security events, and uploading data to the cloud. In order to implement this function, we will collect VIN,  IP Address, and component serial number. Please note that we will not store this data. Additionally, the implementation of the detection system is based on legal requirements or best practice standards..

· Driving Assistance Data:

1) Image Data: In order to implement driving assistance functions such as driving assistance driving (for example, Autonomous Emergency Braking, AEB, Parking Assist service) and driving assistance reversing, the vehicle uses its front, flank, and rear cameras to collect image data of the road during driving or after parking, and image data surrounding the vehicle (e.g., street, lane lines, traffic signs, parking signs.) . Generally, the data from the camera suite is processed directly within your vehicle and will not be transferred to our cloud. 

2) DVR--related Data: Your vehicle may be equipped with a DVR function and you shall comply with all applicable laws, regulations and personal data protection requirements related to the DVR. The DVR video recordings are stored in the local vehicle memory and will not be transferred to our cloud server. When DVR service is turned off, we will not record any video.

· Third-party Service-related Data

To provide you with a better experience, in addition to the functions/services we provide as mentioned above, some services may be provided by third parties (including but not limited to Navigation, Voice Assistant applications, see Section 4 for details). Such third-party service providers will provide and comply with their own privacy statements and shall be responsible for the personal data they collect and process. Please refer to the user agreements and privacy statements provided by these third-party service providers for details. We will also conduct reasonable due diligence to review the personal data protection capabilities of third parties to make reasonable efforts to ensure that they provide services in compliance with legal and regulatory requirements. At the same time, in order to protect your legitimate rights and interests, we suggest that you immediately cease relevant operations and contact us in a timely manner when you discover any risks arising from third-party services.

6. What rights you have in relation to your data

Unless exceptions provided by applicable law and regulations, you generally have the following rights:

· Right of access. This means that you have the right to obtain confirmation as to whether or not personal data concerning you is being processed, and, where that is the case, access to the personal data and the purposes of such processing.

· Right to rectification. If we are processing inaccurate personal data, you have the right to obtain rectification of such inaccurate personal data.

· Right to erasure (‘right to be forgotten’). You have the right to request the erasure of your personal data, subject to certain conditions.

· Right to data portability. You have the right to request a copy of your personal data in order to transmit such data to another controller.

· Right to restriction of processing. You have the right to request restriction of processing your personal data.

· Right to object. You have the right to object, on grounds relating to your specific situation, to the processing of your personal data by us. You also have the right to object to the processing of personal data by us where personal data is processed for direct marketing purposes, unless you want to object to the processing in part.

· Right to withdraw consent. As far as our processing is based on a consent, you may withdraw your consent at any time. The withdrawal of consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing based on the consent before its withdrawal.

· Right to lodge a complaint. You have the right to lodge a complaint with a competent data protection supervisory authority located in your residential country/district.

We hope that we can satisfy any queries you may have about the way we process your data. To exercise your rights regarding the processing of personal data, you can contact us as described below in the section “How you can raise questions about your personal data”. When contacting us and where your issue requires it, we may request necessary information from you, such as to verify identity. 

 

7. How long we will retain your personal data

For data about your purchasing our products or receiving our services, BYD will keep this data for legal and tax preservation obligations. Where you are a customer, BYD will keep your information for the duration of any contractual relationship you have with us, and, to the extent permitted, after the end of that relationship for as long as necessary to perform the purposes set out in this Statement. We retain the information we collect from or about you for the period necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this Privacy Statement unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by law. When the information is no longer necessary for these purposes, we delete it or keep it in a form that does not identify you. When determining this retention period, we take into account various criteria, including the type of services requested by or provided to you, the nature of our relationship with you, the impact on the services we provide to you if we delete some information from or about you, and retention periods required by law.The procedures and methods for destroying personal information are as follows:

l Destruction Procedure

Personal information subject to destruction is identified, and upon approval from the department responsible for personal information management, it is securely destroyed.

l Destruction Method

Personal information stored in electronic file format is permanently deleted so that it cannot be restored. Personal information recorded and stored in paper documents is shredded or incinerated for disposal.

 

8. How we protect your personal data

In accordance with the Personal Information Protection Act, we implement administrative, technical, and physical security measures to comply with relevant legal requirements and take all necessary steps to protect your personal information. The specific measures are as follows:

l Administrative Measures:

- We have established and implemented an internal management plan to protect Personal Data, which is shared with all employees through management approval, emphasizing efforts to protect personal data.

- To manage Personal Data handlers, we collect personal data protection pledges and conduct personal data protection training at least once a year.

- We manage and supervise entrusted companies to ensure that Personal Data is processed securely. Additionally, we regularly conduct personal data protection audits that include these companies.

 

l Technical Measures:

- We control access to Personal Data based on the principle of least privilege, ensuring that Personal Data handlers cannot access unnecessary personal data.

- We encrypt Personal Data using strong algorithms when storing it in databases or transmitting it over communication channels.

- To protect systems and PCs that process Personal Data, we continuously perform security updates and carry out ongoing monitoring to prevent incidents.

 

l Physical Measures:

- Access to data centers and office areas is strictly controlled through access control measures.

- We strictly control access to the computer room and office spaces through access control systems.

- We have installed locking mechanisms in areas where Personal Data is stored to prevent unauthorized access.

 

9. How we will process personal data of minors

We endeavor to protect children's personal data. The definition of children may be different according to the applicable law and regulations (for example, Korea's Personal Information Protection Act has special regulations regarding the processing of personal information of children under the age of 14). The provision of our connected vehicle products and/or services is only for adults, and we will not intentionally collect any personal data from children. In the events when the personal data of the children is collected with the consent of their parents or guardians, we will only use or disclose this data when permitted by law, with the explicit consent of the parents or guardians, or when necessary to protect the legitimate rights and interests of the children. If we discover that we have collected personal data from a child without first obtaining the consent of parents or guardians, we will seek to delete the data as soon as possible. If a parent or guardian discovers that we have collected a child's personal data without the parents or guardians’ consent, please notify us immediately through our call center at 080-808-0008, and we will try to delete the relevant data as soon as possible, except as required by applicable law.
