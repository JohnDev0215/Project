Information Collected from Your Connected Vehicle.

Your vehicle is equipped with various software, sensors, features, and other technologies that may generate, store, transmit or permit the retrieval of information from a vehicle, which includes information related to the performance, location, operation and use of your vehicle and the connected vehicle services. When the connected vehicle services are available and enabled, your vehicle or we will collect your information (Please notice that the provision of the service is subject to the actual function and configuration of the vehicle):

· Vehicle Identifiable Data: Each vehicle has a unique vehicle identification number (VIN). There are other similar vehicle identifiable number, such as vehicle host device ID, vehicle integrate circuit card identity (ICCID) and vehicle international mobile equipment identity (IMEI). Generally, these vehicle identifiable data are not transmitted to the service providers, for example, voice assistant service provider Cerence B. V. , as listed below. These data are only collected if the related services in your vehicle have been activated.

· Vehicle Usage and Performance Data. Information related to the use of your vehicle and the connected vehicle services, as well as vehicle performance, usage, operation, health, diagnostic logs, charging information and service-related data. This includes information such as odometer, mileage, and emissions data; tire pressure data; trouble or error codes, and other diagnostic data; brake status; engine performance;  fuel levels and refueling activity; battery levels and status; vehicle settings, commands and presets, points of interest, and other information about your use of certain features; Wi-Fi password, Wi-Fi security mode, Wi-Fi SSID, Bluetooth name and other performance data; as well as associated date and time stamps for such information. These data can be stored locally in your vehicle and remain inaccessible by us unless a specific event is triggered by your request service or repairs. For example, when you use our car control and monitoring services through BYD APP, we will collect your personal information, including system version, platform version, vehicle door and vehicle door lock status, power gear, status of vehicle windows and sunroofs, tire pressure status, engine coolant temperature,vehicle system warning status, oil pressure indicator light  status, meter fault indicator status, charging plan, charging status, air conditioner type, remaining charging time, whether the air conditioner support temperature regulation, time for remote control of air conditioner, control results of  air conditioner, air conditioner comfort color, PM2.5 concentrations inside and outside the vehicle, driver's seat ventilation and heating status, front passenger's seat  ventilation and heating status, rear seats ventilation and heating status, fuel consumption, cumulative average fuel consumption, charging status, SOC, vehicle light status, in-car temperature, vital signs detection warning, vehicle type, and steering wheel heating status, SN (component identification number), running process information, resource usage information (CPU, memory, etc.), IP address (in car local area network), current system version of the car, and detected security events.

· Vehicle Driving Data. We collect driving data about your vehicle such as speed, acceleration, and braking data; direction of travel; trip data (mileage, date, length, conditions); ignition events; steering events; cruise control data; seatbelt status; information about vehicle incidents or events; other information about how you drive a vehicle; as well as associated date and time stamps for such information. These data can be stored locally in your vehicle and remain inaccessible by us unless a specific event is triggered by your request service or repairs. For example, when you use our car control and monitoring services through BYD APP, we will collect your personal information, including 50km fuel consumption, total mileage, 200m average instantaneous power consumption, and speed.

· Vehicle Geo-location Data. In providing the connected vehicle services, we may collect geolocation information, including your vehicle’s GPS location. When you engage remote services using your mobile device, we also collect your mobile device location information. To protect your privacy, geolocation information is either processed directly without leaving your vehicle or transmitted directly to the service provider in a form that does not directly identify you.

· Diagnostic data. We collect your vehicle operation diagnostic logs for identifying and troubleshooting unexpected software or connectivity issues, and other debugging log reports. Diagnostics may include details about vehicle configuration, firmware, energy use, electronic systems state, and other system-to-system data used to identify bugs and conduct technical evaluations. The readout is generally carried out via the on-board diagnostics (OBD) or the statutorily prescribed connection. These data are stored locally in your vehicle and remain inaccessible by us unless a specific event is triggered by your request service or repairs.

· Infotainment system data. Your vehicle may collect your vehicle infotainment system analytic data regarding infotainment usage such as function successfully or unsuccessfully loaded, aggregated counts of the features or apps used, playback and browsing metadata. These infotainment features can be stored locally, or they can be located on a device that you have linked with the vehicle (e.g., smartphone, USB stick or SD card). These data are only transmitted out of the vehicle  at your request, particularly as part of the use of services in line with the settings selected by you.

· Voice commands: Your vehicle is equipped with the ability for you to use your voice to speak commands to the vehicle. Your voice information will be directly and independantly analyzed by the third-party supplier, and we do not capture or store any voice recordings.  

· E-Call Service: In the event when you are in a traffic accident or you press the SOS button for roadside assistance, the E-Call system on the in-vehicle terminal will provide your vehicle location,contact information and additional required information to the PSAP (Public Safety Answering Point which is set up by government authority). The collection and sharing of these data comply with the requirements of laws and regulations, which only include Minimal Set of Data (MSD), namely MSD message identifier, types of triggers, vehicle VIN code, vehicle power type, timestamp and other required information. Please notice that the provision of this service is subject to the actual function and configuration of the vehicle.

· DMS:  Your vehicle may have been equipped with a camera suite, providing advanced features such as driver monitor system(DMS), which may analyse your behaviours, including your eyebrow and eyelids movement and facial characteristics to determine if you are dizzy while driving and push warning notification. We will not store these data,  please note that such function is based on legal requirement.

· Child Presence Detection(CPD):  In order to detect the presence of a child in the vehicle, we may detect information about the breathing,body movements, or any other sign of life in the vehicle; please note that we do not store this information. In order to sent warning messages, we may collect your vehicle identification number (VIN).

· Cellular Data Usage. The in-vehicle application platform needs to consume cellular data when connecting to the network. In order to  bind, activate data package, provide network services, and facilitate the settlement of data usage fees through querying your data package information and data usage, we need to identify your VIN, Unique Equipment Identification Information (IMEI), Vehicle Integrated Circuit Card Identification Number (ICCID), International Mobile User Identification Number (IMSI), data package, and remaining data usage. In order to provide troubleshooting and analysis services, we need to record data usage (including data usage by each application).

· Intrusion Detection and Prevention Services:  Your vehicle may be installed with intrusion detection and prevention system (IDPS) to provide continuous protection for vehicle from cyberattacks by detecting in-vehicle attacks, monitoring fleet-wide security events, and uploading to the cloud. In order to implement such function, we will collect VIN, Vehicle Intranet IP Address and components serial number. The detection system is based on legal requirement . Please notice that the provision of this service is subject to the actual function and configuration of the vehicle.

· Local Wallpaper and Changeable Themes: In order to realize the model configuration, real-time dynamic and custom settings of the wallpaper, we will collect your VIN, device model, Unique equipment Identification Information (IMEI) , system photo albums and vehicle GPS information. These data are stored in a system local to your vehicle and we do not have access to it.

· NFC Digital Key: To provide you with digital key services (including car key activation, unlocking, and management services), we will collect your VIN, vehicle identifier, Smart Access Controller Cos version, controller serial number, Smart Access controller Applet version, mobile phone system version, cloud service account, key list, key name, pairing password and chip unique identification.

5. What rights do you have in relation to your data?

Unless exceptions provided by applicable law and regulations, you generally have the right to ask us:

· for access to your personal data that we hold.

· withdraw consent given, or deemed to have been in respect of the collection, use or disclosure.

· to update or correct your personal data in order to make it accurate.

If the processing of personal data is based on the consent of the Data Subject, he or she may withdraw his or her consent at any time, but this does not affect the lawfulness of the previous processing.

These rights may be limited in some situations – for example, where we can demonstrate that we have a legal requirement to process your data or if making the information available to you would reveal personal data about another person or if we are legally prevented from disclosing such information. In some instances, this may mean that we are able to retain data even if you withdraw your consent.

We hope that we can satisfy any queries you may have about the way we process your data. If you have any concerns about how we process your data, you can contact us as described below in the section “If you have any questions about your data”.

In the event you still have unresolved concerns, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority, in particular the data protection authority in the Member State of your habitual residence or place of work.

Finally, please note that where we require personal data to comply with legal or contractual obligations, then provision of such data is mandatory: if such data is not provided, then we will not be able to manage our contractual relationship, or to meet obligations placed on us. In all other cases, provision of requested personal data is optional.

6. How long do we retain your data?

For data about your purchasing our products or receiving our services, BYD will keep this data for legal and tax preservation obligations. Where you are a customer, BYD will keep your information for the duration of any contractual relationship you have with us, and, to the extent permitted, after the end of that relationship for as long as necessary to perform the purposes set out in this Statement.  We retain the information we collect from or about you for the period necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this Privacy Statement unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by law. When the information is no longer necessary for these purposes, we delete it or keep it in a form that does not identify you. When determining this retention period, we take into account various criteria, including the type of services requested by or provided to you, the nature of our relationship with you, the impact on the services we provide to you if we delete some information from or about you, and retention periods required by law.

7. How do we protect your data?

We strive to maintain the appropriate standards of security and BYD has put in place robust technical and organizational measures for the protection of your data in accordance with the current, general state of technology, especially to protect the data against loss, falsification or access by unauthorized third persons. However the transmission of information via the internet is not completely secure. So, whilst we will do our best to protect your personal data, we cannot guarantee the complete security of your data transmitted via BYD vehicle . Any transmission is at your own risk. Once we have received your personal data we will use strict procedures and security features to prevent unauthorized access. In the unfortunate event that a personal data security incident occurs, we will report it promptly and take remedial measures in accordance with the requirements of the law and regulatory authorities.

8. Information About Children

We endeavour to protect children's personal data. The provision of our connected vehicle products and/or services are only for adults,, and we will not intentionally collect any personal data from children. In the events when the personal data of the children is collected with the consent of their parents or guardians, we will only use or disclose this data when permitted by law, with the explicit consent of the parents or guardians, or when necessary to protect the legitimate rights and interests of the children.

If we discover that we have collected personal data from a child without first obtaining the consent of parents or guardians, we will seek to delete the data as soon as possible. If a parent or guardian discovers that we have collected a child's personal data without the parents or guardians’ consent, please notify us immediately through the contact data listed above, and we will try to delete the relevant data as soon as possible, except as required by applicable law.
