1. Description of Service

1.1 "Digital Key" is a feature developed by BYD that allows you to register a NFC compatible mobile phone or wearable device as a vehicle key through the BYD digital key registration process and use the mobile phone or wearable device to unlock, lock or start the vehicle. The specific forms and capabilities of the key depend on the actual configuration of BYD models and compatibility of mobile phones or wearable devices.

1.2 The application provides you with digital key management services (including key enabling, key removal and other functions, which may be subject to change. Please stay tuned for our official notice).

1.3 You may go to "Vehicle Settings", click "Door and Window",enter the "Digital Key", click "User Manual" to view the operation method of the application, and the brands and models of mobile phones or wearable devices that support the application.
