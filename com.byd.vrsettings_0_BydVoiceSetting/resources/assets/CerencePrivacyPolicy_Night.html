Cerence Cloud Services Privacy Notice

This Cerence Cloud Services Privacy Notice describes how Cerence generally collects and processes end-user requests through Cerence-designated, third-party, cloud service providers. Use and results may vary depending on customer-specific configurations.


Platform Overview

The Cerence Cloud Services (CCS) platform is Cerence’s multi-tenant, cloud-based speech recognition and infotainment solution. The CCS platform receives end-user requests from a vehicle head unit or a Customer Gateway, processes those requests within the Cerence Runtime Environment and returns a response to the end-user, either directly to the vehicle head unit or through the Customer Gateway.


Information We Collect

Cerence, according to customer’s authorization, collects and processes an end user’s contact list, a customer-assigned external user ID, spoken requests and commands, and uploaded text data (“Service Data”). Cerence pseudonymizes customer external IDs and uses the resulting internal IDs such that data can be linked to a single user but without any directly identifying information about that user. Cerence will not process spoken requests and commands by technical means to generate voiceprints which could be used to identify the data subject.


Why We Process Service Data

Cerence processes Service Data for the following purposes:

  • Provide CCS. Service Data is primarily used to deliver the service, including third-party services that an end-user enables via the CCS, vehicle head units, and APIs. This includes processing to provide responses to end-user requests, to ensure services are working as intended, and to detect and avoid outages or other problems.

  • Maintain and improve CCS. We process Service Data to improve the performance and functionality of the CCS and future embedded models we provide in vehicle head units.

  • Assist customers. We use Service Data when needed to provide technical support and professional services as per customer’s request.

  • Comply with legal obligations and billing. We process Service Data to comply with legal obligations or for billing purposes.

Data Storage

Cerence maintains production servers (“Runtime Environments”) in different regions to process requests from customer vehicle head units. In each region, the CCS Runtime Environment is deployed in a public cloud environment. The provisioning is based on proximity to originating customer traffic to minimize latency and optimize performance. The Runtime Environments as of the date of this privacy notice are in the following locations:

  • North America
    • Public cloud based in Virginia
    • Generally provisioned to handle customer traffic from USA, Canada, Mexico, South America, Central America, New Zealand, Australia, and Asia (excluding China)
  • Europe
    • Public cloud based in Amsterdam, Netherlands
    • Generally provisioned to handle customer traffic From Europe, Russia, the Middle East, and Africa
  • China
    • Public cloud based in Zhangjiakou and Shanghai, China
    • Generally provisioned to handle customer traffic from the China region
  • Japan
    • Public cloud based in Saitama, Japan
    • Generally provisioned to handle certain customer traffic from Japan and Asia (excluding China)

NOTE: Cerence may add to or change these locations as part of capacity planning and process optimization. Cerence will inform customers of such planned changes.

Cerence also maintains a research environment that receives speech data logs from the CCS Runtime Environments for Cerence researchers to use for various CCS improvements. Cerence currently maintains only one research environment in the United States called the Cerence Research Grid (CRG). Data in the CRG is aggregated and pseudonymized from its runtime form.

Cerence also maintains a Business Intelligence platform which is used to handle usage statistics for both customer reporting and billing verification. This platform is maintained in the US and only aggregate data is available to both customers and internal Cerence personnel.

When transferring Service Data outside of the European Economic Area, we comply with the European Commission’s approved framework of model contractual clauses to ensure adequate protection.


Cloud Security

Cerence Cloud Services use strong security features to protect information from unauthorized access, alteration, and disclosure. Specifically:

  • Cerence Runtime Environments are fully protected via VPN-based connections and policy-based access controls to ensure only authorized individuals can access this environment.

  • Cerence utilizes the underlying private network provided by our cloud service provides for transfers between data centers using SDN technologies.

  • The Cerence Runtime Environments store sensitive data, including user profiles and log management systems, on encrypted disks.

  • Cerence regularly reviews its information collection, storage, and processing practices, including physical security measures, to prevent unauthorized access to its systems.

How We Share Data

We do not share Service Data with companies, organizations, or individuals outside of Cerence except in the following cases:

  • When we share information about the Company’s end-users according to customer’s request.

  • When we have the end user’s consent.

  • For processing needed to deliver the Cloud Services. We provide information to our affiliates and other trusted businesses to process it for us, based on our instructions and in compliance with this Privacy Notice and other appropriate confidentiality and security measures.

  • For legal reasons if we have a good-faith belief that access to, or use, preservation, or disclosure of the information is reasonably necessary to comply with applicable law, regulation, legal process, or enforceable governmental request.

Deletion and Retention of Data

  • Cerence maintains speech data logs within the Cerence Runtime Environment for up to 28 days to support debugging and troubleshooting activities. After 28 days, the speech data logs are deleted.

  • Language Model Enrollment data, generally in the form of uploaded contact names, is provided by the customer applications to improve the accuracy of our cloud ASR models. It is purged after six months of user inactivity.

  • Usage metrics from the US-based Business Intelligence platform are maintained for four to five months to provide usage statistics and for billing purposes. The metrics are aggregate in nature and contain no spoken requests or commands. The resulting aggregate reports are maintained for up to 13 months.

  • Service Data is retained in the CRG on a three-year rolling basis. This data allows Cerence to improve the service by training and building iterative AI conversational models and performing error analysis on systemic failures.

Privacy Rights

Cerence is a data processor that according to customer’s authorization collects and processes end-user data according to instructions from the data controller, which is Cerence’s customer. End-users may have rights in connection with the processing of their personal information and should contact the data controller to exercise any such rights.
